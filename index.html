★ Best Daily Detail Sources / Verification Priority

Use this hierarchy for management daily updates. Official NOTAM, AIP and State CAA/AIS sources should be treated as the source of truth for operational decisions. Risk intelligence and news sources should be used to support, explain and cross-check the official position.

1. Official / Source of Truth
  • Current NOTAM Search
  • EASA Conflict Zone Advisories
  • FAA Prohibitions, Restrictions & Notices
  • UK Airspace Security Warnings
  • Relevant State CAA / AIS / AIP pages listed below
2. Daily Aviation Risk Detail
  • Safe Airspace — conflict-zone risk database, country pages and consolidated warnings.
  • OPSGROUP — professional operator daily brief, live ops alerts and airspace-risk intelligence.
  • Use these to identify emerging airspace security trends and affected routes.
3. Breaking-News Verification
  • Reuters Aerospace & Defense
  • FlightGlobal Safety
  • AP Middle East
  • BBC Middle East
Management verification rule: Verify first with NOTAM/AIP and the relevant State CAA/AIS, then cross-check EASA/FAA/UK advisories, then use Safe Airspace/OPSGROUP for consolidated risk intelligence, and Reuters/FlightGlobal/AP/BBC only for breaking-news context.
